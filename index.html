coolbean privacy policy

coolbean is a notebook for your own computer. You run the coolbean server on a computer you control, and the app on your iPhone talks to that server and nothing else.

What the app sends, and where

  • Notes and questions you type, and the words of photos you choose to save as text, go to the coolbean server you set up yourself, over your own Tailscale network.
  • Voice: while you hold the microphone button, the recording goes to that same server, which turns it into text and does not keep the recording.
  • Photos you choose to keep "as the photo" or "Both" go to that same server, which keeps them encrypted with your notes until you forget them.
  • The app sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else. It has no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and it does not use the advertising identifier.

What your own server does with your notes is under your control.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your server's address and the "Speak replies" setting are stored on this iPhone.
  • Notes waiting to be sent while your computer can't be reached are kept in this iPhone's Keychain until they are sent or you delete them.
  • The conversation on screen is kept only while the app is running.
  • When you save a photo as a text note, its words are read on this iPhone. The photo itself is not sent or saved.
  • Sample notes are made up, stay on this iPhone, and are gone when you leave sample mode.

Your choices

  • Leave the address empty and the app sends nothing at all.
  • Delete a waiting note, or forget any saved note, from the app.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on this iPhone.

Contact

Questions about coolbean or this policy: open an issue at github.com/xcelerent/coolbean-privacy/issues.
